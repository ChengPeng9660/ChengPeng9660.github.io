The full edition is an original child-friendly Chinese retelling covering all
36 chapters of Carlo Collodi's The Adventures of Pinocchio (1883). It is not a
word-for-word translation and does not quote a modern Chinese translation.
The earlier six-chapter abridgment remains separately accessible.

33 full-edition chapter illustrations are from the 1902 Italian edition,
illustrated by Carlo Chiostri and engraved by A. Bongini, digitized in Project
Gutenberg eBook 52484. Project Gutenberg identifies this edition as public domain
in the United States; this is not a statement of copyright status worldwide.
Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/52484
Text and plates: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/52484/pg52484-images.html

The chapter 5 and chapter 15 replacement illustrations, the existing six-scene
atlas, and the social-preview image were generated for this project with AI
assistance. Chapter 30 deliberately reuses the atlas carousel as an explicitly
labelled imagined scene. It is not a historical illustration. No new chapter 30
image is represented as generated or reviewed. The original chapter 15 hanging
illustration is excluded from the published assets.

The production asset register and chapter-by-chapter sources are documented in
docs/fullbook/ASSET_SOURCES.md and app/book/art-data.json. Original plate files
retain their native dimensions; they are not described as high-resolution art.
