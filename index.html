I am Cheng Peng, a second-year PhD student in the Department of Decisions, Operations and Technology (DOT) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Zhi Chen and Prof. Wei Tang.

My research interests lie in information design and prediction markets. I am particularly interested in how forecasts can be aggregated and used to support decision-making under uncertainty. I am also actively exploring applications of large language models to forecasting.
