I am Cheng Peng, a researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on forecast aggregation and decision-making under uncertainty.

I am interested in understanding how predictions from multiple experts, AI models, and information sources can be combined into reliable decisions. My recent work studies robust aggregation when the prior, information structure, or underlying state space is unknown, as well as mechanism design for group-fair facility location.
