Prior-Agnostic Robust Forecast Aggregation

Zhi Chen · Cheng Peng · Wei Tang

We study how to aggregate expert forecasts without knowing the prior, their joint information structure, or even the underlying state space. The paper develops an explicit log-odds aggregation rule with near-tight minimax-regret guarantees.

Read paper ↗ Conference
2025 International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems AAMAS 2025
B

Group-fair Facility Location Games with Externalities

Minming Li · Cheng Peng · Ying Wang · Houyu Zhou

We study strategyproof facility-location mechanisms when agents belong to groups and their costs depend on both the facility and other group members. Across competitive and collaborative interactions, we derive improved approximation guarantees and matching lower bounds for group-fair objectives.
